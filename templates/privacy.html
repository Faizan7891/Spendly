Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the following information:

  • Account details: your name and email address provided during registration.
  • Expense data: amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter.
  • Usage data: pages visited and actions taken within the app, used to improve the service.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect solely to provide and improve Spendly. Specifically:

  • To authenticate your account and keep it secure.
  • To display your expense history, summaries, and reports.
  • To diagnose technical issues and improve app performance.

We do not use your data for advertising or sell it to any third party.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. We apply industry-standard safeguards including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access controls to protect your information. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may use third-party services for hosting, analytics, or infrastructure. These providers are bound by their own privacy policies and are not permitted to use your data for any purpose beyond supporting our service. We do not integrate with advertising networks or data brokers.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us. We are committed to addressing your concerns promptly and transparently.
